Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

MyDoseDay is developed by Pentapod. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

MyDoseDay does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter is stored locally on your Mac and never leaves your device.

Data stored locally includes:

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules
  • Dose history logs (taken, skipped, or snoozed)
  • App preferences (theme, sound, snooze duration)

Data Storage

All data is stored in a local database on your Mac using Apple's SwiftData framework, accessible only to the App and your macOS user account.

No Analytics or Tracking

MyDoseDay does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising frameworks, or tracking mechanisms.

No Network Connections

MyDoseDay does not connect to the internet. The App functions entirely offline.

Data Export

The App allows you to export dose history as CSV or PDF files, saved to a location you choose. We do not have access to these files.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent externally.

Children's Privacy

MyDoseDay does not collect information from any user, including children under 13.

Data Deletion

All data is local. You can delete it by:

  • Deleting medications or logs within the App
  • Removing the App from your Mac
  • Deleting ~/Library/Application Support/default.store

Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
