Get help with MyDoseDay.

How do I add a medication?

Click the pill icon in your menu bar, then tap "Add" at the bottom. Fill in the name, dose, schedule, and reminder times.

How do reminders work?

A floating alert appears on screen at each scheduled time with your chosen sound. Mark the dose as Taken, Snooze, or Skip directly from the alert.

Can I set reminders for specific days?

Yes. When editing a medication, choose "Specific Days" under Schedule and select the days you need.

How do I change the notification sound?

Go to Settings and scroll to "Reminder Sound." Select any sound to preview and apply it.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent to any server. Export your history as CSV or PDF from the History section.

How do I delete all data?

Remove the app from your Mac to clear all data, or delete individual items from within the app.

The app is not in my menu bar

Make sure the app is running. Look for the pill icon in your menu bar. If hidden, try closing other menu bar items to free space.
