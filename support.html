Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a medication?

Click the pill icon in your menu bar to open MyDoseDay. Tap the "Add" button at the bottom, then fill in the medication name, dose, schedule, and reminder times.

How do reminders work?

MyDoseDay shows a floating alert on your screen at each scheduled time with your chosen sound. You can mark the dose as Taken, Snooze it, or Skip it directly from the alert.

Can I set reminders for specific days of the week?

Yes. When adding or editing a medication, choose "Specific Days" under the Schedule section and select the days you need.

How do I change the notification sound?

Open MyDoseDay, go to Settings, and scroll to "Reminder Sound." Select any sound to preview and apply it.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your Mac. Nothing is sent to any server. You can export your dose history as CSV or PDF from the History section.

How do I reset all data?

Delete the App from your Mac to remove all associated data, or manually delete the database file at ~/Library/Application Support/default.store.

The app doesn't appear in my menu bar

Make sure the app is running. Check your menu bar for the pill icon. If you have many menu bar items, it may be hidden — try closing other menu bar apps to free space.
