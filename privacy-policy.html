Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

MyDoseDay ("the App") is developed and maintained by Pentapod. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

MyDoseDay does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the App is stored locally on your Mac and never leaves your device.

The data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules you create
  • Dose history logs (when you mark a dose as taken, skipped, or snoozed)
  • App preferences (theme, notification sound, snooze duration)

Data Storage

All data is stored in a local database on your Mac using Apple's SwiftData framework. The database file is located in your user Application Support directory and is accessible only to the App and your macOS user account.

No Analytics or Tracking

MyDoseDay does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising frameworks, or tracking mechanisms. We do not use cookies, pixels, or any form of online tracking.

No Network Connections

MyDoseDay does not connect to the internet. The App functions entirely offline. No data is sent to any server, third party, or cloud service.

Data Export

The App allows you to export your dose history as CSV or PDF files. These exported files are saved to a location you choose on your Mac. We do not have access to these files.

Notifications

The App uses macOS local notifications to remind you about your medications. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is sent externally.

Children's Privacy

MyDoseDay does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The App does not collect any information from any user.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your Mac, you can delete all App data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual medications or dose logs from within the App
  • Removing the App from your Mac, which removes all associated data
  • Deleting the database file located at ~/Library/Application Support/default.store

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
